House of Jewels

Jewel Ghar — “House of Jewels” — bridges two beautiful cultures through handcrafted jewelry. Based in Amsterdam, we blend the vibrant colors, intricate craftsmanship and timeless techniques of Indian jewelry-making with the clean lines and sophisticated aesthetic of Dutch contemporary design.

Each piece in our collection is thoughtfully created to honor both heritage and modernity — for jewelry lovers worldwide who appreciate pieces that tell stories of cultural connection.
